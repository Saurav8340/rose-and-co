Being 5 foot 3 or under has one advantage for satin skirts. They generally look longer than intended, which means more elegant.

The disadvantage is they can look overwhelming if not styled correctly.

## The Amara on petite bodies

Amara skirt is 76 cm from waistband. On someone 5 foot 2:

- Hits at ankle length instead of mid-calf
- Fabric bunches slightly at the bottom
- Skirt looks more traditional (longer plus fitted at waist)

If you like ankle length, this is a plus. If you want mid-calf specifically, you can:

**Option 1.** Get it hemmed. Any local tailor charges Rs 100 to 200 to shorten a satin skirt. Ask them to cut 5 to 6 cm and re-stitch.

**Option 2.** Wear with heels of 2.5 inches or more. The extra height shifts the hem back to mid-calf.

## Styling tips for petite frames

**Wear high-waisted.** The skirt sits at your natural waist and lengthens your legs.

**Tuck in the top.** No blouson tops over satin skirts if you are petite. It swallows your torso.

**Choose smaller prints.** Large scale prints look overwhelming on petite frames. Amara marble swirl scale is medium and works fine.

**Skip long overlays.** Cardigans that hit below the hip make you look shorter. Cropped or waist-length outer layers only.

## What NOT to do

**Do not hem to a mini length.** The proportions of the skirt (waistband, seams) are designed for midi. Cutting too short throws them off.

**Do not wear flat sandals.** Even 1 inch of heel makes a difference on petite frames.

**Do not add belts to define waist.** The skirt already has a waistband. Doubling up on definition looks over-styled.

If you are 5 foot 8 or taller, you already know the problem. Midi skirts are marketed to 5 foot 4 bodies. They hit above your knee on you and look like short skirts.

Here is what to actually look for.

## Skirt lengths, honestly

Most Indian brands measure skirt length from the waistband:

- 70 to 75 cm = knee length on 5 foot 4, above knee on 5 foot 8
- 75 to 80 cm = below knee on 5 foot 4, knee length on 5 foot 8
- 85 to 90 cm = ankle length on 5 foot 4, mid-calf on 5 foot 8
- 95 cm and above = floor length on 5 foot 4, ankle on 5 foot 8

For 5 foot 8 and taller, look for 82 cm or more from waistband.

## Amara skirt length

77 cm from waistband. This means:

- On 5 foot 4 (162 cm): hits below the knee
- On 5 foot 7 (170 cm): mid-calf
- On 5 foot 8 (173 cm): just below the knee
- On 5 foot 10 (178 cm): above the knee

If you are 5 foot 8 or taller and want mid-calf, look for skirts of 85 cm or more. Amara is not the pick for you if you want that specific look.

## Where to find long enough skirts

- Zara: their maxi versions are 90 cm or more
- Reformation: their tall size range is designed for 5 foot 8 to 6 foot
- Massimo Dutti: European sizing runs longer
- AJIO Luxe: some brands here go 90 cm or more

Budget options are harder. Under Rs 2,000, most Indian brands cap at 76 to 80 cm.

## The solution

Buy the standard length skirt and wear it above the knee intentionally. Style it as if it is a mid-length skirt rather than fighting it.

Or buy from brands designed for taller frames.

## The bigger point

Fashion designed for average height does not automatically work for taller bodies. Adjust or shop elsewhere.

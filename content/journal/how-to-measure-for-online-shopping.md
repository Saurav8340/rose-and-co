Wrong size costs you a week — 3 days waiting for delivery, 1 day trying it on, 3 days waiting for exchange. Here's how to get it right the first time.

## You need

- A soft measuring tape (Rs 30 at any tailor shop)
- One friend (optional but helpful)
- 3 minutes

## The three measurements

### Bust

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your bust. Not too tight, not loose. It should sit flat against your body.

Look in the mirror to check the tape is level all the way around (not sloping down at the back).

Note the number in centimetres. If your tape only has inches, multiply by 2.54.

### Waist

Bend to one side. The natural crease at your waist is where you measure.

Wrap the tape around your natural waist (not where your jeans sit, higher up). Loose but not sagging.

Note the number.

### Hip

Wrap the tape around the widest part of your hips (usually 20 cm below your waist).

Not too tight. The tape should slide down slightly if you push it.

Note the number.

## Convert to sizes

The Amara size chart:

| Size | Bust | Waist | Hip |
|------|------|-------|-----|
| XS | 81 cm | 61 cm | 86 cm |
| S | 86 cm | 66 cm | 91 cm |
| M | 91 cm | 71 cm | 97 cm |
| L | 97 cm | 76 cm | 102 cm |
| XL | 102 cm | 81 cm | 107 cm |
| XXL | 107 cm | 86 cm | 112 cm |

Your body measurement should be **2-3 cm below** the garment measurement listed. That's the room for comfort.

## Common mistakes

**Measuring over clothes.** Wear a thin fitted top and leggings. Not jeans, not baggy tops.

**Measuring while breathing in.** Breathe out normally, then measure.

**Not checking that the tape is level.** Ask a friend, or use a mirror.

**Rounding up or down.** Note the exact number. If you're 89 cm bust, don't round to 90.

## When you're between sizes

Between XS and S? Go XS for a fitted look, S for comfort.

Between S and M with a broader chest? S for the top, M for the skirt.

Between L and XL? Always XL. Satin doesn't stretch.

I have been to 11 engagement functions this year. Two rokas in Delhi. Three in Mumbai. One Nikaah in Lucknow. Three sangeets in Punjab. Two in the south. I wore a satin co-ord to five of them.

Here is what works.

## The problem with dressing for engagement functions

Roka and engagement functions sit in an awkward middle. Dressier than a lunch. Less formal than a wedding. Too casual and you look underdressed. Too heavy and you are competing with the bride family.

Lehengas are the safe bet for close family. If you are a friend, cousin, or extended family, you have more room. A satin co-ord fills that gap well if you get three things right.

## One: length matters

If your skirt hits above the knee, it reads brunch outfit, not engagement outfit. Mid-calf or ankle length is the sweet spot.

The Amara skirt sits at mid-calf on someone 5 foot 5 to 5 foot 7. Taller, ankle. Shorter, mid-calf still works.

## Two: colour reads the room

Daytime engagement functions November to February: deep rose, wine, warm ivory, mocha. Jewel-toned but not screaming.

Summer weddings May to August: pale blush, champagne, muted mint. Skip deep colours in heat. You will photograph flat and feel hot.

The Amara marble swirl works for both because it uses three tones. Wine reads formal. Ivory reads soft. Rose ties it together.

## Three: what NOT to add

No dupatta over the set. The print is the pattern. A dupatta over it looks like you are hiding the outfit.

No heavy jewellery. Small hoop earrings, one delicate necklace. That is it. The fabric is the statement.

No heels above 3 inches unless you have practiced. You will be standing for 4 hours. Block heels or wedges in the 2 to 2.5 inch range. Wine, gold, or nude.

No open toe if the function is outdoor and dusty. Closed toe or peep toe only.

## What worked at a Chandigarh roka in October

- Amara set in size M
- Wine coloured block heels, 2.5 inches
- Small gold hoops (2 cm diameter)
- Thin gold chain, no pendant
- Wine envelope clutch
- Hair: low messy bun with two face-framing strands
- Makeup: brown liner, mascara, wine lipstick, no highlighter

Clutch and heels matched the wine in the print. Everything else neutral.

## What did not work

Wore the same set to a Delhi sangeet in August. Two things went wrong.

Delhi summer is 38 degrees. I was standing for three hours. The satin was fine but I overheated. If it is over 32 degrees, wear something breathable or skip satin. Not the fabric fault.

I added a heavy necklace at the last minute. It fought with the print. Should have stuck with the small hoops.

## The bigger point

Satin co-ords work at Indian engagement functions if you treat them as one statement piece. Not a base for more layers. Print does the work. Everything else stays quiet.

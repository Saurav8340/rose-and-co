Small batch means the brand makes a limited number per style, then moves on. No restocks. No 500-SKU catalogues.

Why it matters:
- Better fabric (small production = higher unit cost = better material)
- Less overstock
- Faster iteration on style
- More scarcity = more perceived value

Here are 5 Indian brands that actually do this.

## 1. Bareek (Kolkata)

100-200 pieces per drop. Natural dyes.

## 2. Doodlage (Delhi)

Uses textile waste. Every piece is one-off.

## 3. Rose & Co (Gurugram)

200 sets per drop, then sold out. That's us.

## 4. Nicobar Small Drops

Their limited edition collections cap at 300 pieces.

## 5. Ka-Sha (Pune)

Small batches, handloom fabrics.

## Why small batch is harder

- Higher unit costs (less economies of scale)
- Higher risk of stock-outs
- Can't run big Meta ads because you'd sell out

But it's how real fashion should work. Overstock is the enemy of quality.

Roka function outfits are a specific problem. You need to look dressed for photos but not overdressed. If you are bride sister or cousin, you do not want to pull focus. If you are aunt or older cousin, you want to look pulled together but not too young.

Here is what actually works based on 20 rokas I have observed as a guest or family member across Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, and Chandigarh.

## For the bride sister (age 22 to 30)

**What works:** a satin co-ord, a fitted midi in soft colour, or a plain sharara set with light dupatta.

**What does not:** anything with heavy zari, bright red, or lehengas. That is the bride territory for the day.

**Colours that work:** blush, wine, olive, muted mint, warm ivory, mocha. Never red. Never gold.

**Specific outfit that reads right:**
- Satin co-ord like [Amara](/product/amara-marble-swirl-coord-set)
- Small gold or rose gold hoops
- One delicate chain
- Block heels, 2 to 3 inches
- Envelope clutch in a solid tone
- Hair: soft waves or low bun

## For the bride cousin (age 22 to 30)

Slightly more flexibility than sister. You can wear a stronger colour or a bolder print.

Wine or emerald green work well. Kurtas with light embroidery. Structured co-ord sets.

Skip: anything sequined, anything cream (bridal), anything ankle length in loud colour.

## For the bride mother

Silk kurta or Anarkali in muted jewel tone. Slight embroidery is fine. Heavy is not.

Colours: dusty pink, deep peach, sage, wine, dusty lavender.

Match the wedding style. Traditional wedding, traditional outfit. Modern engagement, modern outfit.

## For the bride aunt (chachi, mausi, bua)

Slightly formal kurta or heavy silk saree. Never lehenga (bride and mother territory).

Colours: teal, aubergine, deep rose, olive, wine.

Practical: if you are standing for 3 hours or more, wear something breathable. Cotton silk over pure silk. Comfortable heels or flats.

## For the bride grandmother

Traditional saree or Anarkali. Nothing to prove. Comfort matters most.

Pale colours: dove grey, pale yellow, dusty pink, powder blue.

## What everyone should skip

- Red (bride colour)
- Ivory / cream / white (bride colour or too casual)
- Gold (bride colour or draws attention)
- Anything sheer (inappropriate for family function)
- Heavy makeup (you are a family member, not the bride)
- New shoes you have not broken in (you will regret it by hour 2)

## The bigger point

At a roka, the bride is the focus. Everyone in her family should support that visually. Coordinated but quieter. Present but not competing.

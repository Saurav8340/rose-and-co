I run both. Meta Ads for scale, influencers for trust. Here's what the numbers say.

## Meta Ads (2025 numbers)

**Cost per click:** Rs 15-40 depending on targeting.

**Conversion rate:** 1-3% for well-optimised D2C fashion.

**Average CPO (cost per order):** Rs 400-800 for satin co-ords in the Rs 1,499 range.

**Break-even:** need 25%+ gross margin, which means selling at 1.5-2x manufacturing cost.

## Influencer marketing

**Cost per Reel:** Rs 5,000-15,000 for micro (5-25K followers), Rs 30,000-100,000 for mid-tier (100K-500K).

**Conversion rate:** highly variable. Best case 5-10%. Average 1-2%.

**Average CPO:** Rs 300-1,500 depending on the influencer.

**Break-even:** faster than Meta Ads because trust is transferred, but harder to predict.

## What works together

Use Meta Ads for volume, cold traffic, scale. Set daily budget of Rs 500-1,000, run continuously.

Use influencers for trust-building and specific audiences.

Best mix: 70% Meta Ads, 30% influencer collabs.

## What doesn't work

- Full budget on influencers only (unpredictable)
- Full budget on Meta only (no trust building)
- Big influencers only (poor targeting)
- Micro influencers only (limited reach)

## For Rose & Co

We spend 80% on Meta Ads and 20% on micro-influencer partnerships.

Micro-influencers get a free Amara set + Rs 3,000-5,000 fee for one Reel and one static post.

Result: CPO around Rs 500-700 blended.

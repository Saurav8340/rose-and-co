You unpacked the satin set. It has creases from folding. You have 45 minutes.

Three ways to fix it without an iron.

## Method 1: Bathroom steam (15 minutes)

Turn shower to hottest setting. Close bathroom door. Hang satin on a hanger inside.

Wait 15 minutes. Steam relaxes creases.

Works best on: fold lines from packaging.

## Method 2: Hair dryer (5 minutes)

Hold satin taut. Point hair dryer at the crease from 15 cm away. Move continuously — never stay in one spot.

Warm air only (not hot). Move the fabric gently while blowing.

Works best on: small localised wrinkles.

## Method 3: Water spray + hang (30 minutes)

Fill a spray bottle with cold water. Lightly mist the wrinkled area.

Hang the satin. Let it dry naturally.

Wrinkles fall out as the water evaporates.

Works best on: wrinkles from sitting.

## What NOT to do

- Never use a hot iron directly on satin
- Never put wet satin in the dryer
- Never wear a wrinkled satin piece to smooth it (you'll add more creases while walking)

Full COD (Cash on Delivery) is dying in Indian D2C.

For buyers, this feels like a loss — COD felt safer. For brands, it was killing margins. Here's what changed and why.

## The old model

Buyer places order. Brand ships. Buyer receives, pays cash.

If buyer refuses (30% do), brand loses:
- Rs 80 forward shipping
- Rs 80 reverse shipping
- Rs 40 packaging
- Rs 200 total per RTO

A brand doing Rs 1,499 sales with 30% RTO = losing Rs 60 per shipment even before making the sale.

## The prepaid model

Buyer pays 100% upfront via UPI. Brand ships. Buyer receives.

If buyer decides to return, they follow standard return process.

Net loss for brand: only actual returns, not "changed my mind at doorstep" RTOs.

## The partial COD model

Buyer pays Rs 299 online via UPI. Rs 1,200 due at delivery in cash.

If buyer refuses, brand loses Rs 200 but keeps Rs 299. Net gain: Rs 99.

This is what many brands (including us) do now.

## For buyers

**Prepaid discount:** Rs 100 off with UPI prepaid (Rs 1,399 instead of Rs 1,499). Enough to change most people's minds.

**Return process:** identical to full-COD returns. 7 days, free reverse pickup, refund in 5-7 days.

**Trust:** if you're worried about the brand, use partial COD. Rs 299 is your protection, plus you see the product before paying the rest.

## The trend

More brands offering prepaid discounts. Full COD is being phased out.

"Hand-printed" and "hand-painted" mean different things.

Hand-printed = pigment applied via a block or screen (block printing, kalamkari).

Hand-painted = pigment applied via a brush.

Truly hand-painted textiles are rare. Here are 5 brands doing it.

## 1. Rose & Co (Gurugram)

Marble swirl print, hand-painted on satin. That's the Amara set.

## 2. Injiri (Delhi)

## 3. Ekaya Silks (Delhi)

Hand-painted borders on silks.

## 4. Bandhej (Rajasthan)

Traditional bandhani tie-dye + selective hand-painting.

## 5. Anokhi (Jaipur)

Block printing that involves hand-painting between blocks.

## What to know

**Colour variance is real.** Machine-printed textiles are identical. Hand-painted ones vary from piece to piece. Yours will look slightly different from the photos.

**Price is higher.** Hand-painting adds 2-3x the labour cost of digital printing.

**Care is different.** Hand-painted pigments are usually more delicate than digital ones. Follow the wash care exactly.

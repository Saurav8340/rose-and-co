## What GSM actually tells you

Grams per square metre. Higher number equals heavier fabric equals better drape equals less clinging when you sit down.

Under 70 GSM, satin looks flat in photos and feels plasticky in person. Between 70 and 90, wearable but not memorable. Above 90, the fabric has weight. You can see it move when you walk. That is what real satin does.

The Rs 500 satin skirt you keep seeing on Instagram ads is 40 to 60 GSM. Photographs fine. Arrives feeling like a shower curtain.

## What to look for in photos

You cannot hold the fabric. But you can see how it hangs.

Good sign: the fabric has natural creases where it folds. Falls in soft rolls off the waistband.

Bad sign: the fabric looks flat and paper-like. No visible weight.

Bad sign: the model is standing perfectly still in every photo. Heavy satin moves. Brands that only use flat lays are hiding thin fabric.

## Two questions to ask the brand

1. What is the GSM?
2. Does the fabric wrinkle when you sit down?

If they cannot answer the first, they do not know their fabric. If they dodge the second, they know and they do not want to tell you.

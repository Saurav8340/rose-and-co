## The two things every satin buyer should ask

**1. What is the GSM?**

Anything under 85 GSM will look and feel disappointing. 90-110 is the sweet spot. Details in our [poly-satin fabric guide](/journal/poly-satin-fabric-guide).

**2. Does the print bond into the fabric or sit on top?**

Digital prints sit on top — you can feel them when you rub the surface. Hand-painted prints bond into the fibre.

We shipped 47 Amara sets in the first 40 days. Once buyers wore them and washed them and stored them, we emailed eight of them asking for honest feedback.

Below is what they said. Only edits are for typos.

## Ananya Verma, 26, Gurgaon &mdash; Size M

Wore it to my cousin roka in Chandigarh 12 days after it arrived. Fabric held up through 4 hours of standing, dancing, and one accidental spill of red wine. The wine came off with cold water in the bathroom in 30 seconds. That surprised me.

Fit was accurate to the size chart. Waistband did not roll down. Crop length was just above my belly button.

Washed once so far, cold water hand wash. Looks the same. Would order another colour if you drop one.

## Sneha Iyer, 29, Bengaluru &mdash; Size S

Ordered Nov 8. Reached Nov 12 to my Indiranagar address. That is 4 days, one working day slower than promised but okay.

Fit is tight through the top for me. I have a slightly broader bust than the size chart accounts for. Skirt fits perfectly. If you are between S and M with a wider chest, size up for the top.

Print is more red-wine than the photos suggested. Prefer the actual thing to the photo. Wore it to Toit for my anniversary.

## Priya Nair, 24, Kochi &mdash; Size M

Delivery was 6 days to Kochi. Expected 4 to 5. Not the end of the world but worth noting.

Fabric is properly satin. My previous Amazon satin skirt at Rs 900 lasted 2 wears. This feels like it will last 20.

One thing though. Skirt is longer on me than on the model. I am 5 foot 2. It is ankle length instead of mid-calf. Like it but note for petite buyers: it will be longer on you.

## Riya Bhardwaj, 27, Mumbai &mdash; Size L

I am 5 foot 8 and usually L in H&amp;M and Vero Moda. Amara L fit me right. Skirt hit mid-calf as the photos showed.

Wore to my friend sangeet in Bandra. Fabric moved beautifully when I walked. Was worried the print would look too much for a wedding function but it landed as a good middle ground.

Waistband is comfortable. Sat through a 4-hour ceremony with no adjusting.

## Meher Kapoor, 25, Delhi &mdash; Size S

S fit me exactly right. I am 5 foot 5, 55 kg, typical S in Zara.

Wore to brunch at Perch in Khan Market with white sneakers. Looked put together without looking overdressed.

Note: the top runs a bit tight through the arms if you have muscular arms from gym. I do not but a friend who does needed to size up to M.

## Tanvi Shah, 28, Ahmedabad &mdash; Size L

L was right for me. I am between M and L in Zara.

Print variance is real. Mine has more ivory and less wine than the site photos. Actually looks more elegant this way. Good surprise.

Kept in a plastic bag in the cupboard for 30 days. No wrinkles when I took it out. Went straight to dinner.

## Sara Menon, 31, Pune &mdash; Size S

Delivery was 4 days to Pune. Standard.

Fit S. Fabric feels premium. Kind of like the AJIO Luxe pieces at Rs 3,500 or above I have bought before.

One month in, colour has not faded. Print looks the same as day 1. Have worn it three times, hand washed twice. Zero issues.

## Nikita Joshi, 23, Jaipur &mdash; Size M

Bought as a gift for my elder sister. She is 30, usually M in H&amp;M.

She loved it. Fit her fine. Wore it to a tilak function.

Only note: the skirt needs one iron the first time out of the box. Small creases from folding. After that, no wrinkles.

## What this tells us

Six sizing observations across eight buyers, five occasions, five cities. Patterns:

- Sizing runs true to Zara / H&amp;M
- If broader in the bust, size up for the top only
- Petite buyers: skirt will be longer, not shorter
- Muscular arms: consider sizing up
- One iron on arrival, then it holds

Not perfect. Nothing is. But nobody said it looked cheap or the fabric felt thin. Which is the honest bar for Rs 1,499 satin in India.

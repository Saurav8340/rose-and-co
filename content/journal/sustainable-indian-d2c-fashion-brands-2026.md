"Sustainable" as a marketing word means nothing.

Every brand claims it. Most are lying. Here's what to actually check and which Indian brands pass.

## The four questions

**1. Do they mass produce?**

Sustainable = small batch. Any brand producing 10,000+ units per month is fast fashion regardless of what their site says.

**2. Do they overstock?**

Sustainable = made-to-order or small drops. If a brand has 500 SKUs available always, they're overproducing.

**3. Do they disclose their supply chain?**

Sustainable brands are transparent about where fabric comes from and who stitches it.

**4. Do they take products back?**

Sustainable brands often have take-back or repair programs.

**Doodlage** — upcycles textile waste into new pieces. Fully transparent supply chain.

**Ka-Sha** — small batch drops, handloom fabrics, no overstock.

**Bareek** — natural dyes, small production runs.

**Anavila** — linen and silk sarees in small batches. Transparent about mills.

**Rose &amp; Co** — 200 sets per drop, no restock. That's us. We fit the small-batch criteria clearly.

**Payal Pratap** — designer wear in small quantities.

**Nicobar** — bigger than the others but disclosed supply chain.

**Kilol** — traditional block printing in small batches.

## Brands that market as sustainable but aren't

Won't name them because legal risk, but here's the pattern:

- Instagram-heavy branding around "conscious fashion"
- Actually thousands of SKUs available
- No supply chain disclosure
- No takeback program

If you see these red flags, check their actual production numbers.

## For a Rs 1,500 satin co-ord

At this price point, truly sustainable is rare. Fabric costs from ethical mills are Rs 400-600/metre. A skirt uses 2 metres. Plus stitching, print, packaging, shipping — the math doesn't leave room for meaningful margin at Rs 1,500.

The [Amara set](/product/amara-marble-swirl-coord-set) at Rs 1,499 is small-batch (200 per drop, no restock) but our fabric is Rs 240/metre poly-satin, not organic silk. Honest about that.

If sustainability is your primary criteria and budget is Rs 3,000+, look at Doodlage or Ka-Sha.

The best dinner date outfits don't look like you tried. They look like this is just how you dress.

Which is a lie, obviously, but the outcome is what matters.

## The formula

One statement piece + everything else neutral.

The statement piece can be:
- A satin skirt
- A slip dress
- A bold coloured top
- Interesting shoes
- One piece of jewellery

Never two statement pieces on the same body. It reads try-hard immediately.

## Five outfits that work

**1. Satin skirt + white fitted T-shirt + heels**
Statement: skirt. Everything else: neutral.

**2. Slip dress + oversized cardigan**
Statement: dress. Cardigan reads casual, undercuts the dressiness.

**3. Fitted jeans + bodysuit + blazer**
Statement: blazer. Structured, effortless.

**4. Midi wrap dress + flat sandals**
Statement: fit. Comfort tells him you know yourself.

**5. Amara top + dark jeans + heels**
Statement: top. Uses one piece of a co-ord set as the print.

## What NOT to wear

**Anything you're pulling down or adjusting.** If the skirt is riding up or the neckline is slipping, you'll be thinking about it all night.

**New shoes.** Every date I've been on where I wore new shoes ended with me faking a smile through toe pain.

**Perfume you haven't worn before.** Test it on a friend day first.

**Statement jewellery.** One earring, one chain, one ring. That's it.

## What actually gets remembered

Not the outfit. What you were like in it.

If the outfit makes you feel yourself, that's the outfit. If you're pulling at it or checking mirrors, wrong outfit.

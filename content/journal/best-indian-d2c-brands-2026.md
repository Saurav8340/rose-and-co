10 Indian D2C brands started in 2023 or later. Not all successful, but all doing something worth watching.

## Fashion

**1. Rose & Co (2026)** — small-batch satin co-ords. That's us.

**2. Bareek (2024)** — natural dyes, small production.

**3. Nomadic State (2023)** — travel-inspired clothing.

**4. Suta (2024)** — handloom, minimalist.

## Beauty

**5. Ras Luxury Oils (2023)** — cold-pressed skincare.

**6. Foxtale (2024)** — direct-to-consumer skincare.

## Home

**7. The Better Home (2024)** — sustainable cleaning products.

**8. Roots (2025)** — home decor from waste materials.

## Food

**9. Wingreens Farms (2023)** — plant-based Indian foods.

**10. Slurp Farm (2023)** — clean baby food.

## What they have in common

- Small teams (under 20 people)
- Direct-to-consumer (no marketplace dependence)
- Focus on 1-2 product categories
- Transparent about supply chain
- Meta Ads as primary acquisition channel

## What kills small D2C brands

- Over-diversifying too fast
- Losing focus on 1 hero product
- Discounting to compete with fast fashion
- Chasing Instagram trends instead of building a brand

The successful ones stay small and specific for at least 2 years.

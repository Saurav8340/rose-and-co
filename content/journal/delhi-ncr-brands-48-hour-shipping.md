Same-city delivery in Delhi NCR should mean 24-48 hours. Most brands promise it. Few actually deliver.

Here are 6 that consistently do.

## 1. Nykaa Fashion (48 hrs)

Wide catalogue. Their own express delivery network. Delhi orders reach next day.

## 2. Myntra (48 hrs on Prime)

Requires Myntra Insider subscription. Nearly all orders next-day if placed before 6 PM.

## 3. Ajio (48-72 hrs)

Less consistent than the two above. Delhi metros usually 48 hrs.

## 4. Rose & Co (24-48 hrs)

We ship from our Gurugram unit. Delhivery pickup at 6 PM daily. Delhi NCR gets it next morning or day after.

## 5. Urban Ladder (48-72 hrs)

For home. Same-city usually delivered in 48 hrs.

## 6. Zara (5-7 days)

Not fast. Included as a note — Zara's India shipping is slower than most local brands.

## The pattern

Local D2C brands with local warehouses beat big marketplaces on speed for the local city.

For Delhi NCR: shop local D2C for weekend delivery. Amazon/Myntra for anything you're happy to wait for.

The line is subtle but obvious once you see it. Confident says "this is who I am". Desperate says "please like me".

Here's what falls on which side.

## Confident (works)

- Fitted midi dress in a solid colour
- Satin skirt + plain tucked-in top
- Fitted jeans + interesting top + heels
- Slip dress with a cardigan or blazer over

Notice: nothing on this list is very tight, very short, or very shiny.

## Desperate (doesn't)

- Anything so tight you can't sit comfortably
- Micro-mini + high heels + statement necklace + bold lip (too many things at once)
- Clothes borrowed for the date
- Heels you can barely walk in

## The rule

Wear what you'd wear to a birthday dinner with your closest friend. Then add one thing.

That one thing is what makes it a date outfit. It could be earrings, better hair, a slightly bolder lip. Just one thing.

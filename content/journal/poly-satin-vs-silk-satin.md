You are deciding between a poly-satin skirt at Rs 1,499 and a silk satin one at Rs 8,000. Both look similar in photos. Both are called satin. Which do you actually buy?

The honest answer depends on three questions.

## Question 1: how often will you wear it?

**Wearing it 8 times a year or more?** Buy silk. It gets better with age. Poly-satin looks the same but does not develop the character silk does.

**Wearing it 3 to 8 times?** Buy poly-satin at 90 GSM or above. Silk is overkill.

**Wearing it once or twice a year?** Definitely poly-satin. Silk needs annual dry cleaning at Rs 300 to Rs 500. If you wear it twice, you spend Rs 500 in cleaning to protect a Rs 8,000 outfit. Math is bad.

## Question 2: how much do you want to think about it?

Silk needs care.

- Dry clean only (Rs 250 to Rs 500 per clean in metros)
- Store hanging, not folded
- Never in direct sunlight (yellows in weeks)
- Never in humidity (mould spots)
- Never near perfume or hair spray (stains)

Poly-satin is forgiving.

- Machine washable (gentle cycle) or hand wash
- Store folded or hanging
- Colour holds through 30 washes or more
- No fussing with humidity or sunlight

If you love the ritual of caring for good clothes, silk is beautiful. If you want to wear it and forget it, poly-satin.

## Question 3: how sensitive is your skin?

Silk is naturally breathable and temperature-regulating. Cool in summer, warm in winter.

Poly-satin does not breathe. In Delhi summer, you will feel it. In Bangalore or Ooty weather, it is fine.

Skin conditions or run hot: silk. Otherwise: poly-satin.

## The visual difference

At 100 GSM, poly-satin looks close to real silk. Not identical, but close.

**Silk sheen:** organic, catches light softly, subtle variation across the surface.

**Poly-satin sheen:** slightly sharper, more uniform, catches light in a more predictable pattern.

At 60 GSM poly-satin, the difference is obvious. At 100 GSM, most people will not tell from photos or from more than 3 feet away.

## The cost of care

Silk over 5 years, wearing 4 times a year, with 4 dry cleans:
- Cost: Rs 8,000 + (4 x 4 x Rs 400) = Rs 14,400

Poly-satin over 5 years, wearing 8 times a year, hand washing at home:
- Cost: Rs 1,500 + Rs 0 = Rs 1,500

For 40 wears vs 20 wears, poly-satin costs 10 times less.

## The verdict

**Buy silk if:**
- Wedding outfit
- Once-a-year statement piece
- You care about how clothing feels
- You have skin conditions
- You can afford Rs 400 per clean without thinking

**Buy poly-satin (95 GSM or above) if:**
- Everything else
- Cocktail parties, dinners, engagements
- You want a piece you can wear repeatedly without stress
- Building a party wear wardrobe on a budget
